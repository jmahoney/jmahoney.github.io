Neal Stephenson: [Writing by Hand is Good for your Brain - Here's how to do it](https://nealstephenson.substack.com/p/writing-by-hand-is-good-for-your). Neal Stephenson has been writing enormous tomes by hand with a fountain pen for over two decades.

Before he went analogue, Stephenson wrote [In The Beginning Was The Command Line](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs81n/command.txt), a wonderful discussion of the benefits of text-based editors and the late, lamented BeOS.

And that reminded me of [this fantastic adventure in writing a graphics driver for BeOS](https://www.haiku-os.org/legacy-docs/benewsletter/Issue4-8.html).
